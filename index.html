What I'm looking for

Putting it out there, sticky-note style.

Ever left a meeting thinking "that could've been an email," right before someone suggests solving it with an AI agent and the whole room nods along? I've learned AI isn't automatically the answer, sometimes it is, sometimes the customer just needs a checkbox, and the interesting part is figuring out which. My personal rule: if "low-hanging fruit," "quick win," and "AI agent" show up in the same sentence, someone has to ask what problem we're actually solving. I use AI every day to prototype and explore, but the best products still start with conversations, not prompts. Give me a messy whiteboard, curious people, and real users, that's where it gets fun. 📝
